[Poem 1: The Broken Mirror]
Language: English
Theme: Self-worth, brokenness, and warning.

I was the silver keeper of your smile,
The shiny truth you trusted for a while.
I held your deepest memories in my glass,
But beauty is a ghost that’s doomed to pass.
Now I lie shattered, scattered on the floor,
The perfect frame you knew exists no more.

Please pull your hand away, don't try to mend,
I will only cut the fingers of my friend.
For when you try to force my pieces tight,
You bleed in vain as i lose my fading light.
To fix me is to deepen your distress,
I am a hazard now, not happiness.

Surrender me to shadow and decay,
The light we shared has nowhere left to stay.
My glory days are gone, my light is dim,
It's my destiny now, forever dark and grim.
Walk away and let the silence descend,
Not every broken thing is meant to mend.
